\section{Machine checks and simulation details}
\textbf{Theorem 1.} Machine checks: 10{,}000 random oblivious schedules (periodic, Bernoulli, burst, diurnal) against the real $F_D$, no violation; periodic $K=1,\dots,64$ and five non-integer periods attain the floor to $10^{-12}$; a schedule with gaps $\{3,17\}$ is strictly above it. Interleaved (Bresenham) schedules attain $\eps^*(a)$ for every $a$ with $aT\in\mathbb{N}$.

\textbf{Lemma 2.} The simulator uses 1-min sub-steps of $n=600$ slots, with constant harvest and rate inside a sub-step, where storage is monotone, so every constraint is checked exactly at sub-step boundaries. The granularity of one activation (cost $r-1$ at once) is covered by adding $r-1$ to fluid storage needs; checked against slot-level simulation at 10\,fps.

\textbf{Lemma 3.} At slot level with integer activations, the greedy schedule equals exhaustive search over $\{0,1\}^T$ ($T\le14$) in 150 random cases.

\textbf{Theorem 3.} The taut-string capacity $C_{\mathrm{TS}}$ is found by bisection with relative tolerance 0.01; the pre-registered check H4b allows $C_{\mathrm{LB}}/C_{\mathrm{TS}}\le1.02$ for this reason.
